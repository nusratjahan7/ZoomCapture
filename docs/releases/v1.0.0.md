The first public release of **ZoomCapture**: a screen recorder for Chrome that zooms in where you click, with a built-in editor and exports to MP4, GIF and images. Everything runs and stays on your computer.

## Install

1. Download **`ZoomCapture-v1.0.0.zip`** below (under **Assets**) and extract it to a folder you will keep, for example `Documents\ZoomCapture`.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the extracted folder: the one that contains `manifest.json`.
4. Pin ZoomCapture from the puzzle-piece menu and click its icon to start recording.

Requires Chrome 120 or newer (or another Chromium browser such as Edge). Full instructions, including how to update later without losing your recordings, are in the [README](https://github.com/nusratjahan7/ZoomCapture#install-from-github-releases).

## Features

**Recording**
- Record your entire screen, a window or a browser tab, with optional camera and microphone, and tab or system audio.
- Countdown, pause, resume and stop; floating controls that are hidden from entire-screen recordings.
- Recordings are saved as they are made, so a crash or a full disk loses at most the last second.

**Automatic zoom**
- Clicks in a recorded tab become zoom effects automatically, following the cursor.
- Every zoom can be moved, resized, retimed or deleted, and new ones added by hand.

**Editor**
- Timeline with video, camera, audio, music, text and voiceover tracks: split, trim, move, snap, undo and redo, autosave.
- Import videos, audio, images and PDF pages; record a voiceover.
- Backgrounds, layouts and aspect ratios (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, custom); camera bubble styles; text, callouts and watermarks; music with fades.
- Subtitles typed by hand, or generated by speech recognition that runs on your computer, exported as .srt or .vtt.

**Export**
- MP4 (H.264 + AAC), WebM, animated GIF, and PNG or JPEG stills, matching the preview frame for frame.
- A settings summary, progress and cancel; large exports can be written straight to a file.

**Library and settings**
- Dashboard of recordings with search, sort, rename, duplicate, download and delete, plus recovery of interrupted recordings.
- Defaults for new projects, camera bubble and automatic zoom; live camera and microphone permission status; storage use and a privacy summary.

## Fixes and improvements in this release

- The timeline ruler stays readable when a selected clip is scrolled under it.
- Fixed a memory leak when an export stopped part-way: a decoded video frame could be left open. Chrome reported this as "A VideoFrame was garbage collected without being closed".
- PDF imports no longer fill Chrome's extension Errors page with harmless font warnings from the PDF library.
- Thicker, easier-to-grab scrollbars that match the dark theme.
- Esc during an export now cancels it, instead of closing the dialog while the export kept running.
- Fewer redraws while editing: dragging a clip now re-renders the editor's buttons about 7× less often (182 → 27 renders over a 20-step drag).
- Pages and editor panels show a recovery screen instead of going blank after an error. A project whose data is missing can be rebuilt from its recording.

## Privacy

No accounts, analytics or uploads. Recordings, projects and settings stay in your Chrome profile. The only network request is the optional one-time download of the speech-recognition model, from Hugging Face, when you first generate subtitles. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/nusratjahan7/ZoomCapture/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).

## Known limitations

- Automatic zoom works for **tab** recordings. Screen and window recordings can't see clicks, but zoom effects can be added by hand.
- Exports run in the editor tab, which must stay open.
- Removing the extension deletes its recordings. Download anything you want to keep first.
- The optional Windows controls helper is Windows-only. Its installer is included in the ZIP (`native\windows\install.ps1`), and ZoomCapture's Settings shows the command to run.

## Checksum

`ZoomCapture-v1.0.0.zip.sha256` holds the file's SHA-256. To check your download:
- Windows (PowerShell): `Get-FileHash ZoomCapture-v1.0.0.zip -Algorithm SHA256`
- macOS: `shasum -a 256 ZoomCapture-v1.0.0.zip`
- Linux: `sha256sum ZoomCapture-v1.0.0.zip`
